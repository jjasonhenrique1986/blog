I recently earned the **AWS Certified Developer Associate**
(DVA-C01) certification and decided to share my study journey and the
materials I used during those 4 months of preparation until I
passed.

## About the exam

The exam has 65 multiple-choice or multiple-response questions,
with 130 minutes when taken in Portuguese
(which I don't recommend). In my case I took the exam in English and got an
extra 30 minutes because English isn't my native language.

The exam costs USD 150, and if you're already AWS certified you get 50%
off your next certification. Currently the exam can be taken
through Pearson VUE and PSI, in person or online.

Below are the domains covered by the exam:

![Table with the AWS Developer Associate exam domains and the weight of each one: Deployment 22%, Security 26%, Development 30%, Refactoring 10% and Monitoring 12%](/images/conteudo-dca.webp)

## My preparation

My main study source was Stephane Maarek's Udemy course,
where I tried to put into practice every lab example that
Stephane runs during the course.

Some points worth highlighting:

-   Understand Lambda's main features;
-   Know how to calculate DynamoDB RCUs and WCUs;
-   Understand how API Gateway works;
-   Understand the ECS components;
-   Master the AWS CLI;
-   Understand when to use exponential backoff;
-   Understand the differences between SQS queues and their options;
-   Understand the different types of Kinesis and when to use each one;
-   Ways to use ElastiCache;

Although the exam focuses on AWS development services,
such as Lambda, API Gateway and DynamoDB, it still covers some services
from the SysOps exam, such as S3, IAM, Parameter
Store, Secrets Manager and KMS.

At the end of my studies I also took two AWS Skill Builder courses that
helped me a lot too. I'll put the links in the references
section.

## Practice exams

The main practice exam I used was Whizlabs', which in my
opinion is the most similar to the real exam, but I also used two other
practice exams I bought on Udemy. Below are the practice exams I used:

- [Whizlabs](https://www.whizlabs.com/learn/course/aws-developer-associate/)
- [Practice exam 1](https://www.udemy.com/course/aws-developer-associate-practice-exams/)
- [Practice exam 2](https://www.udemy.com/course/aws-certified-developer-associate-practice-tests-dva-c01/)

## Conclusion

This was the fourth AWS certification I earned and, so far,
the most fun exam and the one that taught me the most during the
preparation. Coming from an infrastructure background, I had to learn and
dig deeper into some AWS services, such as Lambda, API Gateway and
DynamoDB.
