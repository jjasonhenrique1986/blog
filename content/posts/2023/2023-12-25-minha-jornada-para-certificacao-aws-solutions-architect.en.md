On December 13, 2023 I passed the AWS Certified
Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) certification, so I decided to share
my study journey and the materials I used during that 1
month of preparation until I passed.

This was the second time I earned the AWS Solutions Architect
Associate certification, since I had already taken this exam in 2020, when I was
just starting to work with AWS.

## About the exam

The exam has 65 multiple-choice or multiple-response questions,
with 130 minutes when taken in Portuguese.
In my case I took the exam in English and got an extra 30
minutes because English isn't my native language.

The exam costs USD 150, but if you already have another AWS certification,
you can get a 50% discount voucher. Currently the exam
can be taken through Pearson VUE, in person or online. In
my case, I decided to take it at a Pearson VUE
authorized test center in Campinas (SP), the city where I live.

Below are the domains covered by the exam:

-   Domain 1: Design Secure Architectures (30% of scored
    content)
-   Domain 2: Design Resilient Architectures (26% of scored
    content)
-   Domain 3: Design High-Performing Architectures (24% of
    scored content)
-   Domain 4: Design Cost-Optimized Architectures (20% of
    scored content)

For more information, see the [SAA-C03 exam guide
(PDF)](https://d1.awsstatic.com/training-and-certification/docs-sa-assoc/AWS-Certified-Solutions-Architect-Associate_Exam-Guide.pdf).

## My preparation

My main study source was Stephane Maarek's Udemy course,
where I tried to put into practice every lab example that
Stephane runs during the course. See [Stephane Maarek's Solutions Architect
course on
Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/course/aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate-saa-c03/).

Below are some topics and services worth highlighting that may
show up on the exam:

-   Understand when to use Multi-AZ or read replicas in RDS
-   Understand how to encrypt an RDS instance that isn't encrypted
-   When to use RDS Aurora
-   Understand when to use EKS or ECS
-   Understand when to use an IAM Role
-   Be able to analyze an IAM Policy and identify what it does
-   Understand when to use the different types of Kinesis
-   Understand the difference between CloudTrail and AWS Config
-   Understand when and why to use ElastiCache in your application
-   Understand what the Athena service is for
-   Understand AWS's different database services
-   Understand AWS's main analytics services
-   Understand when to use CloudFront
-   Understand the difference between a security group and a NACL
-   Understand AWS's main container services

## Practice exams

The main practice exam I used was Whizlabs', which in my opinion is
the one most similar to the real exam. See the [Whizlabs Solutions
Architect practice
exam](https://www.whizlabs.com/learn/course/aws-solutions-architect-associate/153).

Another practice exam I recommend for your preparation is the [Solutions
Architect practice exam on
Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/course/practice-exams-aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate/).
